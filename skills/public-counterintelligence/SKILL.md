---
name: public-counterintelligence
description: Research publicly documented foreign-agent, sanctions, corporate and court-record connections without covert identity resolution or surveillance.
---

Inputs: case_id, public organizations and `official_evidence_ids` for attributable
official registrations, sanctions or court records. Preserve citations, retrieval
time, hashes and limits using `investigation_add_evidence`.

Run `investigation_workflow_run` profile `public-counterintelligence` with those
references and optional supported `sources`. Add reviewed typed connections and
competing hypotheses. Inspect contradiction references, temporal validity and
source independence with `source_corroborate` before producing a report.

Expected outputs: official-record appendix, typed organizational network,
alternative explanations and unresolved questions. Espionage-related conclusions
require strong independent corroboration and official records. Missing or conflicting
records mean insufficient evidence. Do not resolve covert identities, surveil,
intrude or conduct operational counterintelligence. Verify integrity and obtain
explicit human authorization for external submission.
